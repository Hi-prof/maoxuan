# Inspirational Reading Materials

Research date: 2026-07-28

The user-facing research deliverable is stored in the repository-root `资料/` directory:

- `资料/README.md`
- `资料/01-毛泽东著作.md`
- `资料/02-毛泽东诗词.md`
- `资料/03-鲁迅与红色经典.md`

The collection contains 51 short candidates grouped around action, perseverance, long-term thinking, courage under difficulty, and relief from anxiety or unproductive rumination. Every entry records a concrete work or speech title, intended editorial theme, verification status, and at least one traceable source link.

Research prioritized full-text pages from the Chinese Marxists Internet Archive for exact Mao quotations, then cross-checked against Communist Party Member Network (`12371.cn`), People's Daily / CPC News, Guangming Daily, China Writers Association, government, procuratorate, education, and military history pages. Internet aphorisms without a traceable original work were excluded.

These files are candidate research only. One reliable traceable source is sufficient for inclusion in the research collection. They do not change the formal `content/` package, whose current validator separately enforces dual-source, series/volume, 90-code-point, interpretation, image-license, and revision checks before publication.
